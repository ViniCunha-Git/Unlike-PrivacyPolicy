Privacy Policy for Unlike

Last updated 4 August 2026

The short version. Unlike has no accounts and no sign-in. Your puzzle progress lives on your device and nowhere else. We do not run analytics and we never see who you are. The only data that leaves your device is what Apple needs to process a purchase, and — if you play the free, ad-supported version — what the advertising network collects to serve ads.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, best times, unlocked and purchased themes, chosen theme, and settings are written to a file inside the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. We have no server, so this data is never transmitted to us and we cannot read it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Unlike offers optional one-time purchases: removing ads, and buying individual themes or the theme bundle. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never receive or store your payment card, billing address, or Apple Account details. We receive only Apple's confirmation that a purchase succeeded, which the app uses to unlock what you bought.

Advertising

The free version of Unlike shows a full-screen advertisement after some completed levels. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect a device advertising identifier and basic technical information about your device in order to select and measure ads.

  • On first use, iOS will ask whether you allow tracking. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. The game itself is identical either way.
  • You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Buying Remove Ads stops all advertising permanently, and with it any data the ad network would have collected.
  • Google's handling of this data is described in Google's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No account, email address, name, or phone number is ever requested.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is embedded in the app.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect any.

Children

Unlike is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It has no chat, no user-generated content, and no social features.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. To erase everything the app has stored, use Settings → Reset all progress inside the game, or delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what data is collected, the date at the top will change and the updated policy will be posted here before the change takes effect.
